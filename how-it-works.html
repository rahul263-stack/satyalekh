    • Versioned rulebook
    • Exact arithmetic
    • Cross-document matching

The claims process

  1. HospitalShares closed claims, redacted

  3. ClaimSetu softwareChecks each cut against the terms
  4. ReviewerConfirms or corrects finding
  5. ClaimSetu softwareDrafts appeal with evidence
  6. HospitalEdits, approves and files it
  7. ClaimSetu softwareTracks the result

The payer's own terms do the work

One rule file per payer holds the policy terms and the rates it agreed with the hospital. The engine works out the most the payer could cut.

Rate agreed with this payer₹75,000
Cut allowed₹17,000

A person settles what the rules can't

Findings with a clear rule go to quick confirmation. Anything without a rule waits for a reviewer. Nothing unclear is appealed automatically.

An appeal with its evidence attached

The draft names the term, shows the arithmetic and lists the documents. The hospital edits it, approves it and files it.

Re: Claim DB-2026-118802 1. Implant. Deducted ₹32,000. The signed tariff supports ₹17,000. Please release ₹15,000. 2. Room and nursing. The discharge summary records a complication. Please release ₹14,000. Total requested: ₹29,000
