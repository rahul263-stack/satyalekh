We'll tell you what isn't ready.

A company that checks records should keep its own straight. This is both products today.

True today

  • Every finding shows the amount, the rule and its source
  • Unclear cases go to a person
  • Nothing is filed with a payer or government portal
  • All demo data is synthetic

Needed before real data

  • Login and roles in ClaimSetu
  • Encrypted storage and an access log
  • Verified payer terms and export rules
  • A signed data agreement and privacy review
  • Testing on real hospital and export file formats
  • End-to-end testing of AI document reading

Questions we expect

Can we send you patient or shipment documents now?

No. Please don't send any. We will ask only once the items above are in place and an agreement is signed.

Does AI decide whether a deduction is wrong?

No. Rules and arithmetic produce the finding, and a reviewer confirms it. AI can help read documents, where switched on.

Are documents sent to an outside AI provider?

Niryat's pilot tool reads locally by default. Its optional Claude mode needs the client's written consent and has not been run on a real document. ClaimSetu has an optional mode that sends uploaded documents to Anthropic's API. It is off unless a key is added, and is untested end to end.

Do you have customers?

Not yet. We are looking for the first hospitals and exporters to learn from.
